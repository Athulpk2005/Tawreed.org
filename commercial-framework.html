Commercial decision framework

Choose the employment model and allocate responsibilities.

The comparison below explains the difference between an agency-employed managed service, direct hiring and an introduction-only recruitment engagement. Actual permissions, service scope, funding, site duties and remedies are established in the agreement. The purpose is to make the decision explicit, rather than assume every outsourcing product transfers the same responsibilities.

Service levels & continuity

Commit to a defined measure and a feasible release.

Standard planning targets — adopted and measured through the service schedule
Service measurePlanning target / provisionClock, dependencies and control
Local mobilisation3–5 working days for a confirmed, ready local cohort.Starts from a complete approved brief and available eligible workers; permits/transfers, assessment, insurance and client access must be complete.
Overseas mobilisation18–25 working days as the cohort planning window.Source-country authorisation, documentation, medical, entry/work route, biometrics, insurance and travel affect the baseline. Authority processing is tracked separately.
Replacement provision90-day replacement review window where included in the signed placement or assignment terms.Specify the start event, qualifying trigger, notification, exclusions, equivalent replacement and agreed remedy. Worker entitlements and lawful due process remain protected.
Emergency reliefPriority triage and a feasible relief roster agreed for the account.Define coverage, response hours, competence and permit/site readiness. A relief pool is reserved only when actual availability is confirmed.
Routine account queryAcknowledge within 1 working day.Incomplete inputs are identified with an owner and required action; safety, wage and immigration risks escalate immediately.
Payroll & service reviewMonthly reconciliation and account review.Report population, period, exceptions, root cause, corrective owner and closure evidence; undisputed wages remain payable by the applicable deadline.

These are service-design targets and contractual provisions, not a historical performance record. The signed schedule fixes the agreed calendar, operating hours, dependencies, client inputs, measurement method and remedy. A timeline never authorises work before the legal and site-readiness gates are satisfied.
