# Corporate Services and ERP Transformation Benchmark

Research reviewed **15 October 2026**, before adding the two services. These are two selected sets of ten established providers with relevant UAE operations, not an independently verified market ranking. The observations below are based on official websites. The resulting Tawreed content is original and reflects the owner's stated services; competitor credentials, clients, scale and partnerships have not been adopted.

## Tax and approval terminology

The owner confirmed both FTA registration and product/health approvals. A blanket “UAE FDA registration” label would obscure the different routes, so the public copy uses the actual tax and regulatory categories.

| Workstream | Primary authority guidance | Wording applied |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Formation and licensing | [Ministry of Economy and Tourism formation framework](https://www.moet.gov.ae/en/establishing-companies) | Mainland/free-zone route assessment, incorporation documents and licensing coordination |
| Corporate Tax / VAT | [FTA Corporate Tax registration](https://www.tax.gov.ae/en/services/corporate.tax.registration.aspx), [FTA VAT registration](https://www.tax.gov.ae/en/services/vat.registration.aspx) | EmaraTax registration readiness and application coordination with authorised parties |
| Food and consumer products | [Dubai Municipality service directory](https://www.dm.gov.ae/dubai-municipality-services/), [ADAFSA food registration through ATLP](https://www.atlp.ae/en/services-landing/regulatory-authorities/request-for-new-food-registration) | Classification and documentation for the relevant emirate/municipality; Montaji where applicable |
| Healthcare and medical products | [EDE healthcare-product authorisation](https://www.ede.gov.ae/en/w/issuance-of-marketing-authorization-for-a-healthcare-product), [Medical-device authorisation](https://www.ede.gov.ae/en/w/issuance-of-marketing-authorization-for-a-medical-device) | Dossier readiness and specialist coordination through eligible licensed/registered applicants |
| Regulated-product conformity | [MoIAT conformity certificates](https://moiat.gov.ae/en/services/issue-conformity-certificates-for-regulated-products), [National conformity marks](https://moiat.gov.ae/en/services/issuance-of-license-certificate-to-use-national-conformity-marks) | Applicable ECAS/EQM evidence, testing and assessment readiness |
| Management-system certification | [ISO conformity assessment](https://www.iso.org/conformity-assessment.html), [ISO certification guidance](https://committee.iso.org/certification.html) | Gap assessment and audit preparation; independent bodies issue certification |

These sources inform terminology, not a legal opinion or an assertion that Tawreed holds regulated-agent, laboratory, certification-body or government-partner status. Actual scope, permitted activities and authorised specialist responsibilities must be agreed.

## HR consultation and HRMS alignment

The owner subsequently clarified that ERP/change work should connect to HR consultation, HRMS implementation and change management, HRMS development, integration and techno-functional advisory. The public service page was revised accordingly; the existing URL is retained so earlier links work.

Additional primary references reviewed on 15 October 2026:

- [KPMG UAE — Building a digital organization](https://kpmg.com/ae/en/services/advisory/management-consulting/people-change/building-a-digital-organization.html): links HR capability, skills and people readiness to digital change.
- [PwC — HR technology](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/services/consulting/business-transformation/workforce-transformation/hr-transformation.html): connects HR/payroll operating processes, platform implementation and adoption. This global service reference supplements the separately verified UAE presence; it is not a new UAE ranking entry.
- [SAP — HRMS definition and scope](https://www.sap.com/resources/what-is-hrms): supports the functional distinction and HR process taxonomy. No SAP partnership or certification is claimed for Tawreed.

The resulting proposition joins HR process diagnosis and policy/workflow design to functional specifications, configuration/development, ERP and attendance/payroll interfaces, migration checks, UAT, role-based training and stabilisation. These are scoped service deliverables rather than guaranteed outcomes.

## Further UAE agency-model review

Following the owner's clarification, the workforce service structure was expanded beyond temporary/on-demand assignments. The homepage now gives separate visibility to recruitment/permanent placement, headhunting/executive search, temporary/contract staffing, manpower outsourcing, EOR/payroll and Emiratisation.

Primary references reviewed again on 15 October 2026:

- [MoHRE agency guidance](https://absher.mohre.gov.ae/en/services/issuance-a-branch-of-the-recruitment-temporary-employment-and-mediation-agency-2022) and [Cabinet Resolution 1/2022, Article 9](https://www.mohre.gov.ae/assets/download/95b8a9d2/Cabinet%20Resolution%20_Executive%20Regulations%20Decree-Law%20No.%2033.pdf.aspx): distinguish mediation, where the agency does not become the employer, from temporary employment/outsourcing, where the agency employs the worker for assignment to a beneficiary. Actual licence, permit and contract must fit the arrangement.
- [TASC services](https://tascoutsourcing.com/en/services) and [permanent recruitment](https://tascoutsourcing.com/en/services/permanent-recruitment-dubai): separate permanent recruitment, contract/temporary staffing, PEO/EOR, payroll and recruitment-process support. Competitor percentages, timelines and guarantees are not reused.
- [Cooper Fitch executive search](https://www.cooperfitch.ae/executive-search): research-led specialist and leadership search.
- [Charterhouse direct recruitment](https://www.charterhouseme.ae/our-clients/client-services/permanent-recruitment): role briefing, candidate assessment and direct-hire support.

The owner also confirmed direct-recruitment capability, technical/IT expertise and a specialist talent database, with discreet references to IT, oil and gas, ports and maritime requirements. These appear in a body paragraph, not the hero, service-card headings, industry badges or navigation. Candidate availability and readiness remain brief-specific; no database size or immediate-availability guarantee is claimed.
