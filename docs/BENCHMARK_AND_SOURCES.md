## Regulatory reference points

- [MoHRE recruitment/temporary-employment agency service](https://absher.mohre.gov.ae/en/services/issuance-a-branch-of-the-recruitment-temporary-employment-and-mediation-agency-2022) and [Cabinet Resolution 1/2022](https://mohre.gov.ae/assets/download/95b8a9d2/Cabinet%20Resolution%20_Executive%20Regulations%20Decree-Law%20No.%2033.pdf.aspx): authorised agency models and employer/beneficiary responsibilities. EOR is not presented as complete client-liability elimination.
- [Government payment-of-wages guidance](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/jobs/employment-in-the-private-sector/payment-of-wages): current page references Ministerial Resolution 340 of 2026. The site uses process-based WPS wording rather than old delay thresholds or unsupported historical percentages.
- [Government Emiratisation guidance](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/jobs/employment-in-the-private-sector/emiratis-employment-in-private-sector): applicability differs by establishment size, activity and period. Outsourced headcount is not assumed to count toward the client’s target.
- [MoHRE company awareness guide](https://www.mohre.gov.ae/assets/92e56bf9/awareness-guide-for-private-sector-companies.aspx): employment and localisation context.
- [Labour accommodation](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/jobs/employment-in-the-private-sector/labour-accommodation): applicable accommodation and registration controls.
- [MoHRE health insurance scheme](https://www.mohre.gov.ae/en/media-center/awareness-and-guidance/health-insurance-scheme.aspx): insurance coverage and implementation context.

Regulatory information is general. Confirm applicable law and requirements before using it in an actual employment or tender decision.
