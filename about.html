Executive vision

Capability, continuity and responsible growth.

Tawreed brings recruitment, staffing, employment administration and enterprise support into one coordinated commercial conversation. Our purpose is to help organisations translate workforce demand into an executable plan: the right employment model, an evidence-based selection decision, lawful readiness, clearly funded administration and accountable support. A permanent appointment, a confidential leadership search and a multi-site temporary deployment require different controls; the operating model should make those differences explicit.

Our vision is consistent with the long-term direction of UAE Centennial 2071 and the Dubai Economic Agenda D33: productive businesses, capable people, competitive services and a stronger knowledge economy. We translate that direction into practical work—genuine skills development, efficient HR processes, disciplined business setup coordination and people systems that produce usable operational information. These national agendas inform our ambition; they do not establish a government endorsement, a programme partnership or a special regulatory status.

The operating philosophy is evidence before assertion. We record the requirement, identify the responsible employer and service owners, document the controls that protect workers and clients, and measure delivery against the agreed statement of work. Where a condition changes, the team records its effect on scope, cost, timing and employee obligations before implementing the change. Commercial disputes are escalated through the account structure while statutory wage and welfare duties remain protected.

Institutional assurance

Management standards that can be examined.

Quality management — ISO 9001

A quality-management workstream connects approved procedures, role responsibilities, service acceptance, document control, non-conformity handling and corrective action. Recruitment files, mobilisation checklists, payroll control reports and client review minutes should identify the version used, the decision owner and the evidence of completion. Recurring failures are reviewed for root cause, with actions tracked to closure rather than merely recording a complaint.

ISO 9001 provides a recognised quality-management reference. ISO now lists the 2015 edition as withdrawn and replaced by ISO 9001:2026; organisations holding a 2015 certificate should confirm transition arrangements with their certification body. This page describes a management approach and evidence requirements. It does not claim that Tawreed holds an ISO certificate.

Occupational health & safety — ISO 45001:2018

ISO 45001:2018 provides a reference for an occupational health and safety management system. In a staffing engagement, the useful operating controls include assignment risk assessment, competent supervision, induction, protective equipment, emergency coordination, worker consultation, incident investigation and corrective-action closure. The client’s control of its premises and equipment is documented alongside the employer’s continuing duties.

A high-risk assignment requires a site readiness review before release, clear stop-work and reporting channels, and a plan for changes in role, location or exposure. Accident response, medical access and insurer or authority reporting routes must be known before work starts. Certification, a camp approval or a client-site audit result is evidenced separately; referencing a standard is not a certification claim.

Information security — ISO/IEC 27001 and UAE PDPL

Recruitment and employment administration involve identity records, bank details, biometric time data, payroll, medical information and client documents. The control design identifies the data controller and processor roles, the purpose of each data set, authorised recipients, transfer arrangements, access permissions, retention and incident escalation. Medical and investigation records require stricter separation than ordinary assignment information.

ISO/IEC 27001:2022 is a framework for an information security management system; the UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 provides a federal personal-data protection framework with scope and exceptions that require assessment. A certificate is meaningful only within its stated entity, service, location and scope. No ISO 27001 hosting certification, fixed encryption suite or deployed security certification is represented for the current static portal.

Questions before engagement

Verify capability through the relevant evidence.

Can Tawreed provide current licences and insurance evidence?

Request the current legal identity, permitted activity, relevant recruitment or outsourcing permission and applicable insurance through the corporate verification channel. The scope and validity of each document must match the proposed service; this website does not substitute for that review.

Are the listed standards certifications?

No certification is asserted without a valid, scope-specific certificate. The governance content describes operating controls and recognised management-system references. A buyer can request its required assurance documents and review any evidenced certification separately.

How is staffing capacity committed?

The account team validates the role mix, source market, selection workload, worker availability and regulatory or site dependencies. A feasible mobilisation plan and governance structure are agreed before headcount or delivery dates become contractual commitments.

HR advisory · PRO · Licensing

Coordinate the HR process and regulatory transaction.

HR advisory begins with the employment model, the actual workforce process and the decisions that need approval. The consultation can review organisation and role structures, recruitment and onboarding, working-time controls, policy application, employee relations, payroll interfaces and manager responsibilities. The output is an implementable responsibility matrix, process map, controlled document or action plan, with legal interpretations referred to the appropriate authorised specialist.

PRO and establishment coordination maps the permitted agency or employer activity, establishment records, quota availability, work-permit and residence dependencies, Emirates ID, medical and insurance requirements, renewals and cancellations. Original identity documents are requested only where an authorised transaction genuinely needs them, with receipt and prompt return. The tracker records the applicant or worker, transaction, authority, responsible owner, evidence, fee, status and next deadline.

Before submission, factual consistency is checked across the licence, offer, contract, employee record and supporting documents. Client instructions do not override authority requirements, lawful occupation, worker rights or a required professional permission. The scope identifies any external legal, tax, immigration, testing or certification representative and the boundary of Tawreed’s coordination. Authority decisions and processing times remain distinct from the consultant’s document-review and response targets.

The account governance cycle reviews expiring documents, transactions awaiting client input, authority queries, payroll or benefit effects and overdue corrective actions. Critical immigration or worker-protection concerns follow immediate escalation; routine requests use the one-working-day acknowledgement target. Approved HR processes can feed techno-functional HRMS development, workflow configuration, migration, ERP integration and change support through the separate HR systems service.

Institutional tenders & supplier evidence

Pre-qualification and ICV are verified processes, not website badges.

Procurement teams can request the current entity, service-permission, tax, insurance and delivery evidence relevant to their scope. A response matrix identifies each requirement, available document, validity, responsible owner and any exception before the submission is authorised. Registration, buyer pre-qualification and tender award are separate decisions; the account team does not present an enquiry or website listing as approved-vendor status.

Where National In-Country Value is part of a tender, the response should provide the actual current certificate and score if held, with the legal entity and certification scope checked. MoIAT’s programme uses authorised certification bodies and evidence such as audited financial statements. Tawreed can coordinate the requested evidence and commercial response; no ICV score, certificate or universal score compatibility is invented.

Tawreed.org is the corporate contact domain. Federal supplier processes use the Ministry of Finance’s official framework, while semi-government and other institutional buyers may maintain their own systems and qualification rules. The buyer’s actual checklist governs document disclosure and eligibility.
