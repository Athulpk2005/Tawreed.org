White-Collar & Professional Staffing

Recruit permanent, contingent and leadership talent through an approved role scorecard, targeted market mapping and structured assessment. Technical evidence, candidate eligibility, references and joining dependencies are reviewed before a recommendation is presented. The account lead coordinates interviews, offer decisions and early placement reviews against the agreed recruitment plan.

  • Permanent & contingent hiring
  • Executive and specialist search
  • Assessment-led shortlists

Industrial, Logistics & Blue-Collar Deputation

Mobilise role-mixed operational teams for project phases, planned peaks and changing client demand. The assignment plan connects trade testing, lawful employment and permit readiness with insurance, site induction, PPE and included welfare arrangements. A named account owner coordinates rosters, attendance exceptions, payroll inputs and controlled changes throughout the deployment.

  • Trade-tested role suitability
  • Readiness and site release gates
  • Shift and account coordination

03 / 08Employer-model review

Employer of Record & PEO Services

Structure eligible UAE hiring through an authorised local employing arrangement with clear client and employer responsibilities. The scope connects employment terms, the permitted visa or work route, insurance, benefits and employee administration before a start date is committed. Monthly governance follows worker status, statutory obligations, service requests and any approved change to the assignment.

  • Contracts and permitted work route
  • Benefits and employee administration
  • Defined employer/client interfaces

04 / 08Maker–checker controls

Turnkey WPS & Managed Payroll Outsourcing

Run payroll through a documented calendar covering employee data, approved time, variable pay, calculation and independent review. Validated salary instructions and SIF files pass through the appropriate approved payment channel with funding and value-date controls. Reconciliation assigns rejected or discrepant payments to an owner and links the result to payslips, benefit records and audit evidence.

  • Approved input and funding calendar
  • SIF and WPS-agent validation
  • Employee-level reconciliation

05 / 08Genuine national roles

Emiratisation — Tawteen & Nafis Solutions

Build an establishment-level national talent plan around real positions, required capability and current MoHRE targets. Sourcing and selection connect relevant Emirati channels with structured assessment, employment documentation and applicable payroll or pension registration. Manager preparation, mentoring and career reviews support retention after the appointment rather than treating recruitment as the end of the programme.

  • Applicability and role planning
  • UAE National candidate pipelines
  • Onboarding and retention support

06 / 08Shift-readiness planning

Facilities Management & Hospitality Manpower

Coordinate on-demand teams around the actual facility, guest-service standard, shift pattern and work conditions. Selection can include practical task testing, service-behaviour assessment, safety awareness and any position-specific eligibility or training. The delivery plan assigns supervision, site access, uniforms or PPE, transport where included, attendance approval and a continuity route for absence or changing demand.

  • Task and service-standard assessment
  • Site, shift and induction planning
  • Attendance and continuity controls
Explore Service Details →
07 / 0818–25-day overseas target

Volume Mobilisation & International Talent Corridors

Plan international recruitment by role, source-country requirements and verified partner capacity rather than a headline headcount. Candidate screening, trade tests, document checks and pre-departure orientation feed a person-level mobilisation register with authority and travel dependencies. Cohort releases are agreed against medical, biometric, insurance, arrival and site-readiness evidence, with exception reporting and staged handover.

  • Licensed partner due diligence
  • Trade tests and pre-departure checks
  • Cohort and arrival readiness tracking
Explore Service Details →
08 / 08HR and regulatory coordination

HR Advisory, PRO & Government Licensing Services

Connect HR operating policy with the employment, immigration and establishment transactions needed to run the workforce. The advisory scope maps responsibilities, approvals, renewal dates, required evidence and authorised submission routes. Account governance tracks outstanding actions, legal or specialist dependencies and a clear handover, while HRMS consultation can translate the approved process into controlled system workflows.

  • HR process and policy consultation
  • Permit and renewal coordination
  • Evidence and escalation ownership
