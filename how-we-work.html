Administration & account ownership

A controlled route through the operating cycle.

One approved mandate and one accountable delivery plan

Administration begins with an approved brief, not the first invoice or a candidate name. The record identifies the contracting entity, employer model, role or project scope, decision makers, financial authority, milestones and evidence needed to start. A KAM consolidates the demand register, dependencies, commercial assumptions and escalation contacts so the client can see which decisions affect the plan.

The mobilisation file distinguishes client instructions, employment decisions and authority requirements. Missing information is returned with an owner and due date; a critical worker, safety or wage issue is acted on immediately rather than waiting for a complete routine form. Version control links each approved change to the live scope and roster.

Coordinate the employment record and the client assignment

Where employment administration is included, the record connects the approved offer and contract to work authorisation, insurance, start date, assigned location, reporting lines and payroll. Joining, transfers, salary changes, leave, renewals, assignment extensions and separation each require an authorised effective date and supporting evidence.

The client confirms work and factual time through the agreed supervisor, while the employing entity controls the employment actions assigned to it. Permit and insurance expiries, site access, worker queries and change requests remain visible in an exception register. The employee has a named contact and a private route for concerns.

Keep the wage control separate from invoice recovery

The account calendar establishes time and variable-pay cut-offs, independent payroll checking, client approval interfaces, funding, salary-file submission and reconciliation. A change to bank details or pay requires an authorised request and independent verification. The employer’s wage obligation is managed against the applicable deadline even where a client invoice, purchase order or timesheet dispute remains open.

Billing then reconciles the contracted rates, approved headcount or hours, joiners and leavers, included benefits, pass-through charges and authorised adjustments. The account review separates a commercial discrepancy from an employee entitlement. Both receive owners and evidence, with escalations appropriate to the risk.

Review the exceptions and close the actions

The proposed monthly account review covers recruitment progress, deployment readiness, attendance and payroll exceptions, permits, insurance, worker welfare, service requests, commercial changes and planned demand. A dashboard defines the population, source, reporting date and measurement method so a percentage or count can be interpreted correctly.

Actions are assigned to named client and Tawreed owners with a due date and closure evidence. Serious safety, wage, immigration or data issues bypass the monthly cadence and follow immediate escalation. Repeated failures require root-cause review and a verified corrective action, while a scope change is approved through the commercial authority matrix.

The four operating pillars

01 / SERVICE PILLAR

Professional recruitment & executive search

Use direct recruitment when the requirement is a permanent appointment and the client will become the employer. The mandate establishes position outcomes, reporting relationships, compensation parameters, essential credentials and decision authority before candidate outreach begins. Executive search adds confidential market mapping, targeted approaches and controlled disclosure for leadership or scarce-skill appointments. Technical and IT staffing expertise is applied within this wider role-led search model, drawing on the specialist talent database and confirming availability for each assignment.

The selection file connects structured behavioural interviews with work-relevant technical assessment, identity and qualification checks, references where authorised, and documented reasons for recommendation. Candidate information is shared for a defined recruitment purpose with appropriate privacy notices and proportionate access. A shortlist contains assessment evidence, material gaps, remuneration expectations and realistic notice or joining dependencies. Client interview feedback, conditional offers, acceptance and start confirmation are tracked through the agreed recruitment plan. Post-placement reviews address role fit, onboarding progress and any concerns within the contracted replacement terms; they do not replace the client’s employer responsibilities.

02 / SERVICE PILLAR

Temporary staffing & managed manpower

Access capacity for short assignments, recurring demand, extended contracts or complex phased programmes without treating every requirement as a permanent vacancy. The operating brief defines headcount by role, work location, shift pattern, assignment duration, supervision, competence, safety exposure and measurable service acceptance. Where the lawful model is temporary employment or outsourcing, the permitted agency-employer arrangement and beneficiary responsibilities are confirmed before a person is released to work. Scale and speed are planned against actual candidate readiness, approvals and site capacity.

Sourcing may combine screened local availability, approved talent pools, trade or task testing and authorised overseas recruitment partners where appropriate. The mobilisation tracker records contract and permit status, medical and insurance dependencies, induction, PPE, accommodation or transport if included, and client access. Four readiness gates prevent a roster target from overriding lawful deployment. During the assignment, a named account owner coordinates timesheet approval, payroll changes, attendance exceptions, worker contact and replacement requests. Ramp-up, redeployment and demobilisation require written change control so cost, statutory obligations and operational continuity remain visible.

03 / SERVICE PILLAR

EOR, PEO & payroll administration

Separate the need to employ or administer people from the decision to establish and operate an internal employment infrastructure. An eligible EOR engagement uses an authorised UAE employing arrangement; a payroll-only engagement retains the client as legal employer. PEO and managed HR scopes are defined by the actual contract and jurisdiction, rather than assuming a foreign co-employment model applies automatically. The discovery review identifies the employing entity, work location, permit route, activity restrictions, benefits, funding and allocation of daily supervision.

Administration then links the signed employment terms to employee master data, approved time and variable pay, independently checked payroll calculations and the applicable WPS payment channel. Bank or agent responses are reconciled to the approved register and employee queries are assigned to a case owner. Leave, insurance, pension eligibility where relevant, end-of-service accruals and separation instructions form part of the monthly control cycle. The commercial schedule separates pay and statutory pass-through costs from management fees, deposits and agreed benefits. Client invoice disputes are handled separately from worker wage obligations; no arrangement removes every liability or every market-entry obligation.

04 / SERVICE PILLAR

Emiratisation & UAE National development

Treat Emiratisation as an establishment-level workforce plan that must create and sustain real employment. The initial review confirms the relevant MoHRE establishment, its size, skilled-role composition, applicable activity classification and current official target. Nafis candidate and support mechanisms are considered separately from the employer’s obligation to recruit, pay, register and develop eligible UAE Nationals. A placement is evaluated for substantive contribution, with duties, manager ownership, work location and evidence of actual work defined before the offer.

Candidate pipelines can include Emirati graduates, experienced professionals, technical specialists and leadership candidates. Screening connects career objectives and role requirements with structured interviews, job-relevant assessments and verification appropriate to the position. Onboarding coordinates employment documentation, applicable pension registration, payroll readiness, manager briefing and a practical learning plan. Early-career support, mentoring and periodic career conversations address integration and progression instead of relying on recruitment alone. Account reviews follow retention, attendance, role fulfilment and open actions. Contribution exposure and official target status are checked against current government records; outsourced headcount is not represented as automatically satisfying a client’s Emiratisation quota.

Illustrative engagement scenarios

A delivery model for different forms of demand.

These scenarios explain how a mandate can be organised. They are illustrative operating designs, not named client testimonials or reported project outcomes.

01 / DIRECT RECRUITMENT

A client needs permanent capability in the team.

The requirement is a direct appointment, with role outcomes, selection criteria and ownership of the employment relationship agreed from the start.

Execution
Define the role, source and assess candidates, coordinate interviews and checks, then support the offer and client onboarding handover.
Controls
A documented shortlist, selection feedback and agreed placement terms.
02 / CONFIDENTIAL EXECUTIVE SEARCH

A specialist appointment calls for targeted outreach.

The client needs a researched approach to suitable candidates, including people who are not actively applying for roles.

Execution
Agree the target profile and confidentiality protocol, map relevant talent, approach candidates and coordinate structured assessment.
Controls
Candidate consent, controlled communications and an evidence-based shortlist.
03 / SHORT-NOTICE CAPACITY

A time-bound requirement arrives with little notice.

The client needs additional capacity for a defined period, with different competencies and a clear end date.

Execution
Confirm the brief and lawful deployment route, check availability and readiness, and agree a staged release instead of an unsupported start-date promise.
Controls
Readiness sign-off, attendance coverage and a named escalation owner.
04 / MULTI-SITE COORDINATION

One requirement. Several locations and shifts.

A client needs coordinated teams across locations, with separate access rules, reporting lines and shift patterns.

Execution
Create a site-by-site allocation plan, confirm supervision and access, and use one consolidated roster with local handovers.
Controls
Site acceptance, shift reconciliation and an agreed replacement route.
05 / PHASED & COMPLEX MOBILISATION

The team changes as the project progresses.

Different role groups are needed at different milestones, with controlled ramp-up, handovers and planned demobilisation.

Execution
Sequence the role mix, document release gates and dependencies, and align training, payroll inputs and workforce changes to the project plan.
Controls
Change approval, milestone readiness and documented offboarding.
06 / RECURRING ON-DEMAND SUPPORT

Demand varies. The control framework stays consistent.

The client needs repeat access to temporary capacity as schedules and work volumes change.

Execution
Agree call-off rules, availability confirmation, assignment lengths and cut-offs; maintain an approved escalation and continuity plan.
Controls
Confirmed assignments, reconciled time records and service-review actions.

Institutional supplier readiness

Qualification follows evidence and buyer requirements.

Government procurement is the controlled process through which a public body registers suppliers, evaluates capability and purchases goods or services. Semi-government buyers may operate their own supplier portals, qualification rules and tender procedures. A federal supplier registration, a buyer-specific pre-qualification and a contract award are separate stages; none can be inferred from the wording of a company website.

Tawreed can structure a supplier-response pack around the requesting organisation’s requirements: legal identity and permitted activities, authorised signatories, tax registration evidence, relevant employment permissions, service methodology, commercial schedules, data handling, insurance, welfare controls and supporting declarations. Documentation is shared through an agreed verification route and checked for currency, scope and consistency before submission. Where the buyer requires an audited result, reference or certification, only the corresponding verifiable evidence should be used.

For federal, semi-government and tier-1 contractor enquiries, the account team translates the tender into a compliance matrix with an owner, evidence reference, exception and submission deadline. The corporate website tawreed.org is Tawreed’s contact domain; it is not a government procurement authority or an official public procurement standard. No supplier registration, approved-vendor listing or tender eligibility is asserted here.
